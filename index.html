[ 03 / ABOUT ]

Hi. I'm Danilo. Full stack developer who spent a decade shipping enterprise platforms and never stopped building things that blink, scroll, and occasionally crank.

By day I write Java, TypeScript, and whatever the sprint demands. By night I solder ESP32 boards, prototype Playdate games, and load film into a Nikon FM2n I bought on impulse. I work with a tiny crew under the name mtgm mtgm . Otherwise I'm probably debugging a breadboard.
